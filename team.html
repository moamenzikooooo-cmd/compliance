How these figures are worked out
  • Number of reports: distinct reports (date + storer + type + checker + mail name) in "Total Accuracy Collect Reports".
  • Productivity: sum of Total Checked for every line, repeats included. Occupied includes the Discrepancy lines, so Occupied + Zero = 100%.
  • IRA WH coverage: IRA-counted locations ÷ Location List locations (fake excluded). With all months picked, it is the total across the months shown.
  • Missing TRNS: active days in the calendar × storers that send TRNS, with no TRNS line, outside WH Exceptions.
  • Pending TRNS: transaction date later than, or the same as, the last TRNS count date, or never counted in TRNS.
  • Wrong data: count of distinct reports listed in the Wrong Data tab.
  • Targets: reports and productivity targets come from the Target tab, summed for the storers and months in the filter, up to the last month with data. A warehouse without a target is left out of the % of target. Targets for Missing TRNS and Pending TRNS are 0; for IRA coverage it is 100%.
