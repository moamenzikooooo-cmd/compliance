How these figures are worked out
  • Stock value: sum of "Total cost" (on hand × cost price) from the Total DMG-EXP tab, split by Status (Damage / Expired).
  • Qty with no price: on-hand units whose Cost Status is "No Cost Price" ÷ all units of that status.
  • Expired age: months between the expiry date and the data date.
  • Case flow: QA not done → step 1; QA done and CRM not done → step 2; CRM done and Finance not done → step 3; Finance done → closed. "free", "no" and "yes" count as done.
  • Cause: from the mail title first (rain / water / leak → Rain; fall / break → Handling), otherwise from the Reason column (raining → Rain, incident → Incident, printing area → Printing area); empty → Not stated.
