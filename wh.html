How these figures are worked out
  • Total checked / accurate locations overall: sum of Total Checked / OK in "Total Accuracy Collect Reports" (IRA only: report type starting with IRA).
  • Discrepancies on OPS / others: lines in "All Discrepancies_2026" by Responsible Department: Ops Team, WMS Team, VAS Team = OPS; every other department = others. Month comes from the discrepancy date.
  • Final accuracy: (Total checked − discrepancies on OPS) ÷ Total checked.
  • QTY accuracy: (Total checked − QTY issues) ÷ Total checked. Data accuracy: (Total checked − data issues) ÷ Total checked.
  • Target: 99.85% accuracy. Colours: 99.95% and above dark green, 99.85–99.95% light green (on target), 99.70–99.85% yellow, below 99.70% red.
  • In progress: a month marked * is not complete yet, so its figures will still change.
